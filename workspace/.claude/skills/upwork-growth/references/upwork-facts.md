# Upwork facts snapshot (checked 2026-10-04 — verify before relying on them)

- Freelancer service fee: variable 0%–15%, shown when submitting a proposal and fixed for the
  life of that contract (replaced the flat 10% fee from 2025-05-01).
  Sources: https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026 ,
  https://www.upwork.com/resources/is-upwork-free
- Connects: about $0.15 each; most jobs cost 6 Connects, competitive ones 10–16.
  Freelancer Plus: about $19.99/month with 100 Connects.
  Source: https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-connects-promo-code
- Badges: Rising Talent is typically reachable within ~90 days for active new accounts;
  Top Rated needs 90%+ Job Success Score and at least $1,000 earnings; Top Rated Plus needs 95%+ JSS.
  Earning a badge adds bonus Connects (reported 30 each, up to 90).
  Source: https://gigradar.io/blog/upwork-connects-promo-code
- Upwork publishes no public job RSS feed for freelancers anymore; jobs are browsed in-app.
